Interactive prototype

ChicLifts

A women-only place to start moving: events from clubs, creators and brands, coaches who welcome beginners, and challenges with rewards from local businesses.

Try this

  • Answer the three start questions to get a first step picked for you.
  • Join an event, then use the demo check-in to earn kilometers and a brand perk.
  • Tap any club, creator or brand to see its profile.
  • Open Profile, then Partner view, to see what a host sees and create an event.
  • Open Challenges and connect Strava, then tap Sync now to watch your steps count toward rewards.
  • Open any event or coach to see where it happens, then browse the Shop for new sports brands. Open People to switch between coaches and creators, and Discover to find healthy places to eat with member discounts.

Who gets what

Members
Free to join and use

Coaches

All brands, creators, numbers and codes are invented sample data, with original logos. Launch market: United States, with a Spanish version planned.
